Alice Johnson traveled to Paris in July 2020 for a business conference. 
She met with Thomas Okafor from Nigeria and Dr. Emily Brown from the University of London. 

The meeting covered global health research, vaccine development timelines, 
and new AI-based diagnostic tools.

Later, the group visited the Eiffel Tower and discussed upcoming partnerships 
planned for 2021 and 2022.

Overall, the trip was considered a success by the entire research team.
